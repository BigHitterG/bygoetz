# Current Lulu Production Notes Used in This Brief

Checked August 4, 2026. Reconfirm before final upload because platform specifications may change.

- Lulu Help Center, “Tips for Formatting Documents”: Hardcover Casewrap minimum 24 pages and maximum 800; odd pages print on the right; page counts divisible by four reduce the possibility of printer-added blanks; Lulu accepts interior files as single pages rather than two-page spreads.
- Lulu Help Center, “What is Full Bleed?” and “PDF Creation Settings”: a 6 x 9 inch full-bleed interior uses 6.25 x 9.25 inch pages; artwork extends 0.125 inch beyond trim; important content stays at least 0.5 inch inside trim; allow additional gutter room; no trim or bleed marks.
- Lulu Help Center, “Printing: The Basics”: Standard Color is intended for limited color and is not recommended for image-heavy pages; Premium Color is the safer recommendation for a fully illustrated picture book.
- Lulu Help Center, “Interior Formatting: The Basics”: 300 PPI is the optimal image resolution and sRGB is recommended for full-color interiors.
- Lulu Help Center, “Mandatory Print Book Distribution Requirements”: for Global Distribution, omit spine text on books under 100 pages; title and copyright metadata must match; cover and interior are separate PDFs.
- Lulu Help Center, “Creating Your Hardcover Casewrap Cover” and “PDF Creation Settings”: use the custom template generated for the exact page count, paper, and casewrap; hardcover critical content requires a generous safety area.
