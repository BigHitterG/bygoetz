# Gromas and the Gobbledygooks
## Fourteen Final Interior Spread Prompts

These are complete prompts, not shorthand scene notes. The manuscript text is included for layout reference only and must not be rendered inside the illustration. Individual prompt files are also located in `prompts/spreads/`.

## Correct physical pagination

The title page is page 1 on the right. Copyright is page 2 on the left. Page 3 is a dedication/prelude on the right. The first true story spread is therefore pages 4–5, followed by 6–7, 8–9, and so on through 30–31. Page 32 is a final colophon/end spot.

## Spread 1: The Power Crisis — Pages 4–5

**Narrative purpose:** Introduce Gromas, the Gobbledygook community, the hidden under-floor world, and the fading Great Power Supply.

**Locked manuscript text**

```text
Gromas stood and addressed the group
About the adversity facing the troop.
“Energy levels are low,” he said with fear.
“The end of the Great Power Supply is near.”

“What’s the plan?” someone shouted aloud,
From a distant spot in the curious crowd.
“Our scientists, after much research and talking,
Discovered a way to harness their walking.”
```

**Text-safe zones:** Left page upper-left; right page upper-right. Keep both zones calm, pale, and low-detail.

**Narrative purpose:** Deliver the wordplay, reveal that the energy source is the human couple upstairs, and introduce Chet as the inventive second-in-command.

```text

Who do you speak of—the King and the Queen?”
“No, you wing wang rompus anew,
The people upstairs in the apartment for two!”

“Here is the device; it’s called a Spinette.”
“Gazoo! Gazoo!” They all looked to Chet,
For Chet was the second in command,
And they knew he’d have a flandiferous plan.
```

```text
“For when they step down with their feet so large,
Whiz buzz spinaroo—Eureka! A charge!
Zippy zap zap, zap zap-a-roo!
The Great Power Supply will be good as new!”
```
