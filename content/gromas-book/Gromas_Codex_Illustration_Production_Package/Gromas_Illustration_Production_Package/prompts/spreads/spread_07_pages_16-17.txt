ART-ONLY TWO-PAGE CHILDREN’S PICTURE-BOOK SPREAD. Do not render the manuscript text inside the image.

FORMAT AND COMPOSITION
- Landscape 4:3 master composition designed to span two 6 x 9 inch portrait pages.
- Final trim spread is 12 x 9 inches; preserve a quiet vertical gutter zone around the exact center.
- Important faces, hands, mechanisms, gauge readings, and story actions must not fall within approximately 0.45 inch of either side of the center seam.
- Extend backgrounds and noncritical texture fully to the outer bleed.
- Leave the designated text-safe zones low-detail, with sufficient tonal contrast for separately typeset verse.
- Read visually from left to right.

LOCKED VISUAL LANGUAGE
- Whimsical, cinematic children’s picture-book realism with watercolor and gouache texture, delicate graphite-like linework, subtle colored-pencil detail, soft atmospheric light, and selectively vignetted paper edges.
- Richly observed miniature environmental storytelling, warm humor, and believable scale.
- Hand-painted and literary, not glossy CGI, plastic toy rendering, flat vector art, anime, or photorealism.
- The Gobbledygooks live in a tiny handmade civilization inside the floor cavity beneath an ordinary apartment: wood joists, floorboards, salvaged platforms, copper wiring, brass gauges, glass, ceramic insulators, fabric scraps, ladders, tools, and warm buttered toast.

GROMAS — LOCKED MAIN CHARACTER
Use references/gromas_reference.jpeg as the authoritative design source. Gromas is a friendly moss-green Gobbledygook with a body covered in long rounded ribbon-like organic strands, large round cream-colored eyes with warm brown irises, a tiny black mouth and no visible nose, rounded green limbs, four-digit hands, broad three-lobed feet, and a soft tactile surface. He is kind, capable, earnest, and expressive. Preserve his silhouette, eye spacing, colors, limb proportions, and face in every spread. His wooden broom-like staff or pointer is optional and should appear only when compositionally justified.

RECURRING OBJECTS
- Spinette: a harmless shallow circular under-floor pressure generator with a compressible plate, spiral cam or rack, central copper coil, small flywheel, ceramic insulators, and two copper leads. It must not resemble a weapon, mine, trap, or modern commercial product.
- Great Power Supply: a consistent handmade brass, copper, glass, and wood apparatus with a prominent vertical power bar that can read low, empty, or charging through light level and fill height.

NO-TEXT RULE
Do not create letters, words, title type, speech bubbles, labels, equations, readable numbers, page numbers, signatures, logos, or watermarks. All text will be added later as real vector typography.

SPREAD-SPECIFIC BRIEF
Create a cinematic cross-section of the apartment floor structure during the installation. The left page begins in daylight: Lookouts occupy tiny forts and press listening horns to pipes and floorboards; Frontliners saw, hammer, clamp, and mount Spinettes beneath selected footpaths; Electricians inspect the Great Power Supply and trace the hidden copper grid. The right page transitions into deep night: lamps glow, shadows lengthen, the same operation continues, and exhausted workers pause for quick bites of buttered toast before returning to work. Show fatigue through posture, drooping body strands, and heavy eyelids, but keep the tone courageous and child-safe. Gromas moves among the teams offering encouragement rather than force. Chet checks a connection with a small meter. Above the floorboards, the apartment should be empty and still; do not introduce the humans yet. The scene should feel like a miniature architectural section, with joists, subfloor, platforms, ladders, and wires clearly organized. Use full-bleed watercolor/gouache with precise observational detail and a day-to-night gradient. Do not place a character face, cutting blade, or critical wire joint in the gutter.

TEXT-SAFE ZONES
Left page upper-left; right page upper-right. Keep the upper floor surfaces relatively simple.

QUALITY EXCLUSIONS
No extra limbs, missing limbs, extra fingers, fused hands, detached hands, duplicated Gromas, mismatched eyes, changed character colors, random clothing changes, floating tools, broken perspective, garbled mechanisms, or inconsistent scale. No horror, slime, gore, grotesque teeth, claws, menace, weapons, exposed dangerous electricity, rats, insects, neon cyberpunk, generic Victorian steampunk costumes, heavy sepia, over-saturated plastic surfaces, or clutter behind the text. Do not crop Gromas’s face or key emotional gestures unless specifically directed.
