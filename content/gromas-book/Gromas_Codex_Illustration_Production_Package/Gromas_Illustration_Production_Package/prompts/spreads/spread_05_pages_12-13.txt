GROMAS — LOCKED MAIN CHARACTER
Use references/gromas_reference.jpeg as the authoritative design source. Gromas is a friendly moss-green Gobbledygook with a body covered in long rounded ribbon-like organic strands, large round cream-colored eyes with warm brown irises, a tiny black mouth and no visible nose, rounded green limbs, four-digit hands, broad three-lobed feet, and a soft tactile surface. He is kind, capable, earnest, and expressive. Preserve his silhouette, eye spacing, colors, limb proportions, and face in every spread. His wooden broom-like staff or pointer is optional and should appear only when compositionally justified.

RECURRING OBJECTS
- Spinette: a harmless shallow circular under-floor pressure generator with a compressible plate, spiral cam or rack, central copper coil, small flywheel, ceramic insulators, and two copper leads. It must not resemble a weapon, mine, trap, or modern commercial product.
- Great Power Supply: a consistent handmade brass, copper, glass, and wood apparatus with a prominent vertical power bar that can read low, empty, or charging through light level and fill height.

NO-TEXT RULE
Do not create letters, words, title type, speech bubbles, labels, equations, readable numbers, page numbers, signatures, logos, or watermarks. All text will be added later as real vector typography.

SPREAD-SPECIFIC BRIEF
Create a rich workshop montage spanning one continuous under-floor factory space. On the left page, Electricians carefully build the hidden grid: they thread copper wires through tiny porcelain or ceramic insulators, solder connections at brass junctions, test faintly glowing bulbs, and route the network along joists so the wires are concealed. On the right page, Frontliners assemble and test rows of identical Spinettes on wooden benches, compressing plates and watching coils turn. In the upper and far-right background, Lookouts build compact forts and observation posts from matchstick-like lumber, fabric scraps, thimbles, and salvaged household objects. Include a comically serious stockpile of warm buttered toast protected under cloths and cooling on racks. Gromas and Chet move through the scene checking work; do not duplicate them multiple times as a time-lapse. The Great Power Supply should remain visible in the distance with a low, declining bar. Make the environment detailed enough to reward repeat viewing, but preserve clear focal groups and open text zones. Use a handmade mechanical aesthetic—brass, copper, wood, fabric, and glass—without generic Victorian steampunk costumes or excessive gears. Full-bleed painterly illustration, warm workshop gold against deep green-blue shadows.

TEXT-SAFE ZONES
