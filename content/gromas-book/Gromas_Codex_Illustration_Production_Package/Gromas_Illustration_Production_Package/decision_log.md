## Open illustration-critical editorial point

The line **“He waved to the troops and said, ‘Spindection!’”** follows Chet’s invention speech, so the nearest grammatical antecedent is Chet. The production interpretation in this package is therefore:

- Chet presents the Spinette, says “Spindection,” and assigns the three teams.
- Gromas remains beside him as the overall leader and later leads the installation effort.

The manuscript remains unchanged. For absolute textual clarity, the authors may later approve the minimal change **“Chet waved to the troops…”**. Do not make that change automatically.

## Format note

The earlier rough outline paired pages 3–4, 5–6, and so on. In a bound book, true spreads are even-numbered left pages paired with odd-numbered right pages. This package corrects the sequence so the first story spread is pages 4–5.
